When non-technical domain experts launch a tech startup in Finland, they face a common decision: **Should you hire a fractional CTO (CTO as a Service), hire a software consultancy, or partner with a Technical Co-Founder Studio?**

In Finland's competitive startup ecosystem, choosing the wrong engineering model can result in wasted capital, delayed launches, or equity disputes.

Here is a direct breakdown of how **CTO as a Service** compares to a **Technical Co-Founder Studio** like Pomegroup Studio.

---

## 1. Defining the Models

### What is CTO as a Service?
A fractional or interim CTO provides high-level technical guidance, software architecture advice, and vendor selection for an hourly or monthly retainer (€100 - €250/hour in Finland).

*   **Pros**: Strategic advisory without committing long-term equity.
*   **Cons**: A fractional CTO **does not write code** or build your application. You still need to hire external freelancers or agencies to build the software.

### What is a Technical Co-Founder Studio?
A venture builder studio (like [Pomegroup Studio](https://pomegroup.studio)) acts as both your **CTO advisor AND your full-stack engineering team**:

*   We handle system architecture, UI/UX design, and code execution.
*   We build and deploy the Minimum Viable Test (MVT).
*   We invest technical capabilities in exchange for shared equity partnership.
