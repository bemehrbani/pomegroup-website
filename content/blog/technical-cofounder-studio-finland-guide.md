## The Challenge: Finding a CTO in Finland's Tech Ecosystem

Finland has one of the world's most vibrant startup ecosystems (home to Slush, Business Finland, and world-class engineering talent). However, senior technical talent is highly sought after:

1. **High Opportunity Cost**: Experienced Finnish senior developers and software architects often earn top salaries at established tech companies or scale-ups.
2. **Equity Misalignment**: Many prospective co-founders hesitate to join pre-seed ideas full-time without a clear product build strategy or initial funding.
3. **Slow Matchmaking**: Searching for a co-founder through networking events or matchmaking portals can take 6 to 12 months—costing valuable time-to-market.
