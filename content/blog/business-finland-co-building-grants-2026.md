For early-stage startups in Finland, **Business Finland** provides vital non-dilutive grant funding (such as Tempo, Sprint, and R&D grants) to accelerate market testing and international scaling.

However, a recurring reason applications are delayed or rejected is a lack of **technical execution credibility**—specifically, having a domain founder without a technical CTO or clear software development roadmap.

Here is how partnering with a **Technical Co-Founder Studio** strengthens your Business Finland grant eligibility and speeds up execution.

---

## 1. What Business Finland Evaluates in Technical Applications

When reviewing early-stage startup funding proposals, Business Finland evaluators focus on three core pillars:

1. **Innovation & Competitive Advantage**: Is the SaaS, AI, or deep-tech product genuinely novel?
2. **Market & Customer Validation**: Can the team run rapid market tests with real target customers?
3. **Execution Capability**: Does the founding team possess the technical skills required to build and deliver the solution?

If your founding team consists solely of business or domain specialists without dedicated software engineering, evaluators may question your ability to deliver the technical milestone on schedule.
